📖 Our Story

Building Agricultural
Excellence Since 2004

Mindanao AgriPlus Corporation is a direct importer and national distributor of organic and inorganic fertilizer, chemical fertilizer, and pesticides.

Our company was founded in 2004 as a rice, sugar and copra trader. Through the years, we have added fertilizer, pesticides, and other commodities as we see the growth and potential of the agriculture industry.

We value our reputation, honesty, and commitment to our suppliers, clients and our business partners. We take pride in having passed the quality test for our products as we continually provide excellent products.

Mission

To discover, develop, provide, and deliver innovative products essential to the agricultural industry. To serve our customers with excellent quality products.

Vision

We value our shareholders, suppliers, and customers, committed to work with excellence and uplift our community by actively participating in community development.

Culture

We listen, understand, support, uplift, and work together as a team.

Faith

All these are possible with the loving guidance of our Lord Jesus Christ whom we commit our talents and abilities for His Honor and Greater Glory.
